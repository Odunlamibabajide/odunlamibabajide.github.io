# Project Shield — PCI DSS Compliance Readiness (AltSchool Capstone)
**Mandate:** Bring CartNova Technologies Ltd. to PCI DSS v4.0 compliance within 4 months on a $13,500 budget.  
**Strategic Approach:** Implement SAQ-A Scope Reduction.

---

## Compliance Timeline Strategy

### Month 1: Discovery & Scoping Mapping
*   Map Cardholder Data Environments (CDE).
*   Perform PCI DSS v4.0 gap assessments.
*   Analyze vendor processing pathways, confirming SAQ-A eligibility requirements.

### Month 2: Partner Verification & System Hardening
*   Collect and verify Attestation of Compliance (AOC) documents from Paystack and AWS.
*   Isolate network segments, configure Web Application Firewalls (WAF), and enforce multi-factor authentication (MFA).

### Month 3: Control Implementation & Staff Training
*   Remediate remaining technical gaps and execute data minimization policies.
*   Establish central system log audits, run staff security awareness courses, and perform external penetration testing.

### Month 4: Audit Validation & Program Handover
*   Review and submit completed SAQ-A documentation.
*   Conduct internal mock audits, present compliance briefs to the board, and transition ownership to operations.

---

## Strategic Scope Reduction Architecture
By fully outsourcing payment transactions to a compliant third-party gateway (via secure iFrame/redirect pathways), the merchant's network never handles, processes, or retains primary cardholder data. This reduction reduces direct audit checks from several hundred down to under thirty.
